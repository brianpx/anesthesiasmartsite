Journal Club

Resident-led critical appraisal for CA-2 anesthesiology. Each session ties a paper to a clinical decision. Search the sessions by date, topic, or presenter.

How a session runs

A session is a discussion, not a lecture. A BLUF is the bottom line up front: one sentence on what you would do differently, or the same, because of the evidence.

Sixty-minute session agenda
TimeWhat happensWho leads
0–5 minWelcome, objectives, and housekeepingFaculty
5–15 minBLUF for each article, about a minute or twoResident leads
15–30 minValidity and results, walked through the checklistGroup
30–45 minApplicability, using a clinical vignetteGroup
45–55 minDoes this change our practice?Group
55–60 minTake-home points and anything to follow upFaculty

Preparing a paper

Start with the question, the design, and the result that would change a plan. Then ask whether the conclusion is supported, and whether it applies to the patient in front of you. The blank handout is the same checklist used in the sessions above.

Critical appraisal series

Seven parts from the resident appraisal curriculum. This page is the map. It is not the facilitator script.

  1. Foundations, PICO, and the EQUATOR network. Why appraisal changes a clinical decision, and how a vague question becomes a PICO with a reporting guideline beside it.
  2. Randomized trials and CONSORT. Randomization, allocation concealment, blinding, and reading the size of an effect instead of stopping at a p-value.
  3. Observational studies and STROBE. Cohort versus case-control, and confounding, selection, and measurement.
  4. Systematic reviews, PRISMA, and figure forensics. How a review is assembled, and how to read a forest plot without taking the summary estimate on faith.
  5. GRADE. Moving from one study to a judgment about certainty.
  6. AI-assisted research. What still has to be checked when a model helps search, screen, or draft.
  7. Machine-learning studies. The reading for this part is Liu et al., How to read articles that use machine learning, JAMA 2019. PubMed
